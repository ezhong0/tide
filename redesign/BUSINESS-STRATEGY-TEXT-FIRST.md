## Executive Summary

**Tide** is an AI-powered chief of staff that lives on your phone, handling executive tasks with the intelligence of a senior assistant and the availability of software.

Unlike simple AI chatbots or basic productivity tools, Tide orchestrates complex workflows across email, calendar, and tasks while maintaining full context of your professional life. It doesn't just respond to commands - it anticipates needs, protects time, and handles routine decisions autonomously.

**The Opportunity**: 8.5 million executives and senior professionals globally spend 15+ hours weekly on administrative tasks. At $200/hour implied cost, this is a $170B annual productivity loss.

**The Solution**: A mobile-first AI that acts as a true chief of staff - making decisions, handling communications, and managing complexity so executives can focus on what matters.

**The Business**: $150/month subscription targeting executives, senior professionals, and ambitious managers. Path to $100M ARR within 24 months.

## Product Vision

### The Experience

#### Morning (7:00 AM)

You wake up to a gentle notification:

**Tide**: "Good morning. Three important items for today:
1. Board deck needs final review (I've flagged 3 issues)
2. Conflict at 2pm - I suggest moving product sync to tomorrow
3. John from Acme needs response - I've drafted three options

Your 9am is confirmed. Brief ready. Coffee shop is 5 min from office."

With three taps, you've reviewed the deck changes, approved the calendar change, and sent the response to John.

#### Commute (8:15 AM)

In the car, you speak naturally:

**You**: "Tell the team I'll be 10 minutes late and start without me. Also see if we can move the Goldman meeting to next week."

**Tide**: Shows visual confirmation of both actions. Sends the message. Finds three slots that work for everyone at Goldman. One tap to confirm.

#### During Meetings (10:30 AM)

Your phone sits silent, but Tide is working:
- Declining non-urgent requests
- Drafting responses to important emails
- Preparing for your next meeting
- Watching for genuine emergencies

#### Evening (8:00 PM)

**Tide**: "Today's wrap: Handled 47 emails (3 need your review), rescheduled 2 meetings, completed project brief. Tomorrow is lighter - want me to protect 2 hours for deep work?"

### Core Capabilities

#### 1. **Intelligent Email Management**
- Reads, understands, and responds autonomously
- Drafts in your voice
- Knows who matters and why
- Handles routine responses without you

#### 2. **Strategic Calendar Control**
- Optimizes your entire schedule
- Protects focus time
- Handles all scheduling negotiations
- Prepares briefs for every meeting

#### 3. **Relationship Intelligence**
- Maintains CRM of every professional contact
- Tracks commitments and follow-ups
- Reminds about important relationships
- Suggests touchpoints

#### 4. **Decision Engine**
- Queues all decisions needed
- Provides context and recommendations
- Executes approved actions
- Learns from your choices

#### 5. **Workflow Automation**
- Detects repetitive patterns
- Suggests automations
- Executes multi-step workflows
- Coordinates with team members

### Design Principles

1. **Powerful, Not Simple**
   - Surface simplicity, deep capability
   - Progressive disclosure of power
   - Expert mode available

2. **Beautiful at Every Touch**
   - Pixel-perfect interface
   - Smooth animations
   - Thoughtful microinteractions
   - Premium feel

3. **Trustworthy by Design**
   - Show reasoning
   - Preview all actions
   - Undo everything
   - Perfect track record

4. **Anticipatory, Not Reactive**
   - Solve problems before they arise
   - Prepare for what's coming
   - Learn and adapt
